A menu you can play with

Mock-up for the Shift Jam title menu, layout A. Nothing here is in the game yet. Try each way of playing with it, then say what to keep.

click fires · drag stretches · G swaps the gun · arrows move · Enter fires · Esc goes back

Click: a dart

A click fires a foam dart at that spot. A button it hits thunks, squashes and is chosen, and the dart stays stuck. Hit a letter of the title and it shifts to another colour set.

Drag: a stretch

Hold and pull a button or a letter and it stretches on an elastic, then snaps home and bumps its neighbours. Pulling never chooses, so it is safe to play with.

G: the dart LMG

Swap the blaster for a dart LMG and hold the button to spray. It knocks everything about and sends the title's colours spinning, but it never chooses a button: that takes the blaster, or Enter.

Shift

F swaps the whole menu between toy view and kid view, with the title's colours rippling as it goes. The game's own idea, used on its menu.

Lettering shapes and colours are the game's pixel title. Button captions use a stand-in pixel font. Sounds are placeholders made in the browser.
