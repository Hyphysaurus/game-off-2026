- **D-0006 (2 Nov, 21:1x) Jump clips are retimed to the motor, not the other way round.** His play read
  as stutter and jitter. Found in the files: the blend tree played Idle/Walk/Run at a tenth speed; the
  Jump idle start clip (1.42 s) played whole at speed 1 while the capsule had already left the ground at
  frame 0 (the whole hop lasts 0.62 s); no fall path, so walking off an edge played the crouch; the
  capsule's isGrounded flicker fired the jump branch; every landing went out through the sub-machine
  Exit and restarted the blend tree. Now: clip speeds 1; starts enter at an offset and play at 2.5 / 2;
  `VerticalVelocity` splits jump from fall; moving landings blend straight to Locomotion; end clips are
  cut by any input; direct transitions only; `groundedGrace` 0.1 s in the motor. Also: Mario-style
  variable jump (`jumpCutMultiplier` 0.5, release early = short hop; 1 turns it off). The controller
  keeps his sub-machine layout and fileIDs; it was regenerated by script (scratchpad gen_controller.py)
  with Unity closed. Why: with physics-first movement the clips must follow the capsule; a 24 fps pack
  clip authored for a slow showcase cannot drive the jump.

- **D-0010 (3 Nov, 07:0x) The shift's look is a fullscreen warp of our own, driven by the Shifter's progress.**
  `Shaders/ShiftWarp.shader` (HLSL, URP Full Screen Pass after post-processing): a ring grows from the
  screen centre (grow) or collapses into it (shrink); inside it the picture is pushed radially with the red

  front, which no stock post-process override does; 80 lines of HLSL, one material slider set to tune.

- **D-0013 (3 Nov, 11:4x) Shift cores are batteries; the flashlight is the device.** His idea, resolved: cores
  sit inside powered things and are dormant until the thing is on; the flashlight holds one (spent after
  its single grow) and is the one core you can carry. Any size presses its switch (E on the body); a kid
  picks it up (E), aims it by looking, toggles it (Attack: LMB / X / Enter) and puts it down (Crouch: C / B),
  resting it on whatever is under the hand, pointing where the kid faced. A lit beam wakes a dormant core
  it rests on for 0.4 s (LightSensor: cone, range, line of sight), and the core stays awake. No collector
  that stores charges: fixed cores are the level design (reach it as a kid, reach it as a toy); the carried
  light keeps the "collect and harness" feel without free shifting. Rules live on the things: `Flashlight`
  (switch + static list of lit lights), `Carryable`/`Carrier` (hands, never parenting so the 35x scale stays
  off the thing), `LightSensor`, `ShiftSource` dormant/uses/looks. The Interactor skips targets whose prompt
  is empty, so a spent core never takes E from the body beside it. Box starts dark (ambient 0.3, lid on,
  flashlight off) with a dim ember at the tail: light is the awakening. Dark-room navigation is his later
  idea; the on/off switch already supports it.
