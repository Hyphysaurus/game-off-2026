The menu that shifts from 2D to 3D

Second mock-up for the Shift Jam title menu. It opens flat, like the pixel title. Press F (or the Shift button) and the same menu pops out into 3D: the title turns out to be made of little blocks, the buttons are slabs hanging in the room, and darts stick where they land. Nothing here is in the game yet.

Room for the gun

The gun sits in the bottom right corner and turns to follow your aim, so it never covers a button. The six buttons keep the middle to themselves.

Shift is 2D to 3D

It is one scene the whole time. In 2D it is seen dead flat through a very long lens. The shift pulls the camera in while the lens widens, so the menu keeps its size on screen and the depth appears around it.

Depth you can see

Buttons tilt and show their sides when hit, cast shadows on the wall, and the scene leans a little as you move the mouse. Missed darts stick in the wall.

What the game would use

Your Blender slabs, the game's own foam dart and the weapon pack's gun models. The shapes here are stand-ins made of boxes.

Lettering shapes and colours are the game's pixel title. Captions use a stand-in pixel font. Sounds are placeholders made in the browser.
