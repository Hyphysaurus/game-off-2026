# Decisions (ADR log; newest at the end)

- **D-0001 (2 Nov 2026) Three buckets.** `Assets/_Game` ours, `_Imports` bought content, `_Tools` bought tools.
  Why: one glance tells ours from theirs; the licence split follows the folders.
- **D-0002 (2 Nov) Licensed packs stay out of git.** ithappy packs, Feel, Shapes, Fast Script Reload, Linework
  Lite are ignored wherever they sit (`**/` rules, after two moves broke path rules). UVCS carries them; the
  public repo holds only our work and Unity's own template content. THIRD_PARTY.md says how to restore.
- **D-0003 (2 Nov) Shift = scale shift.** Toy 1:8 vs kid 1:1 in one room. Over a day/night world shift and
  a kid-to-car form shift. See DESIGN.md.
- **D-0004 (2 Nov) Code stack.** Own motor and shoulder camera (no Cinemachine: portals need clean warps,
  and 80 + 100 lines are readable whole); the template Input System asset; ScriptableObjects for tuning
  values only, never as event channels; plain C# events; no assembly definition for the jam; Core scene +
  additive worlds arrive with the first portal, not before.
- **D-0005 (2 Nov) Who does what.** Claude writes and explains every script (landed as files, compiled
  outside the editor first); Maram does the editor side (character, animator, room, feel values) and the
  design calls.
- **D-0006 (2 Nov, 21:1x) Jump clips are retimed to the motor, not the other way round.** His play read
  as stutter and jitter. Found in the files: the blend tree played Idle/Walk/Run at a tenth speed; the
  Jump idle start clip (1.42 s) played whole at speed 1 while the capsule had already left the ground at
  frame 0 (the whole hop lasts 0.62 s); no fall path, so walking off an edge played the crouch; the
  capsule's isGrounded flicker fired the jump branch; every landing went out through the sub-machine
  Exit and restarted the blend tree. Now: clip speeds 1; starts enter at an offset and play at 2.5 / 2;
  `VerticalVelocity` splits jump from fall; moving landings blend straight to Locomotion; end clips are
  cut by any input; direct transitions only; `groundedGrace` 0.1 s in the motor. Also: Mario-style
  variable jump (`jumpCutMultiplier` 0.5, release early = short hop; 1 turns it off). The controller
  keeps his sub-machine layout and fileIDs; it was regenerated by script (scratchpad gen_controller.py)
  with Unity closed. Why: with physics-first movement the clips must follow the capsule; a 24 fps pack
  clip authored for a slow showcase cannot drive the jump.
- **D-0007 (2 Nov, 22:3x) Every jump uses the run-jump clips.** The pack's idle-jump clips squat deep before
  launch, then hold a tight tuck in the air; on a 0.6 s hop that reads as a cannonball, and no offset fixes a
  held pose. The run-jump clips launch at once and cycle gently in the air (he: the running jump "looks decent").
  Locomotion now routes any take-off (VerticalVelocity > 0.5) to Jump run start at offset 0 and any fall to Jump
  run middle; the idle states stay in the controller unused, two transitions away from coming back. Fingers for
  later (D-00xx when weapons land): the rig has 2-joint fingers on both hands; grip = one-frame pose clip on a
  hand-masked layer + a Grip socket per weapon + left-hand IK for two-handers.
