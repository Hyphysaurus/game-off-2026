Driving HUD fonts and icon: where they come from and their licences

Teko-Bold.ttf        Copyright 2023 The Teko Project Authors (https://www.github.com/googlefonts/teko)
                     SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in Teko-OFL.txt.
Fredoka-SemiBold.ttf Copyright 2016 The Fredoka Project Authors (https://github.com/hafontia/Fredoka-One)
                     SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in Fredoka-OFL.txt.

Both are Google Fonts. They were taken out of the ModularUI Asset Store package, but the OFL lets them be shared, so
these two TTFs, their OFL texts and the TextMeshPro font assets made from them (Resources/HUD/Teko SDF.asset and
Fredoka SDF.asset, made by Shift Jam > UI > Make HUD Fonts) may sit in the public repo. Keep each OFL text next to its
font. Nothing else from ModularUI is in the project; the rest of that package stays out of the repo.
