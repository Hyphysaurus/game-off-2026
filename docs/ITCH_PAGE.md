## Description
✍ **[HIS PITCH: a few lines in your words, what it feels like to play]**

A toy figure shifts between toy size and kid size. Each room is played at both sizes: small, the floor is the level;
big, the furniture is. From the cardboard toy box, through the bedroom, along the skirting board, across the playroom
and through the wardrobe to the window.

**Controls:** WASD + mouse or a gamepad. E uses things and shifts you at a glowing core. Esc pauses. H shows every key.

**Made for** GitHub Game Off 2026 (theme: Shift) in Unity 6.
