## Description
✍ **[HIS PITCH: a few lines in your words, what it feels like to play]**

You are a toy who can shift between toy size and kid size. Small, the floor is the level and a sock is a wall; big,
the furniture is the level and the toy soldiers are underfoot.

**Alone:** wake up in the cardboard toy box, learn the moves, the dart gun and the shift in the hallway, and make your
way through the house: the bedroom, along the skirting board, across the playroom and through the wardrobe to the
window ✍ (check the route still reads this way since the one-house rebuild, 20 Nov). The playroom has doors to the Raceway (toy cars: drifts, nitro, three laps against rivals) and the Colour
Corner (paint a grey room back to colour against the clock).

**With friends (online, up to 4):** host a party or join one from the list or with a code. Fight it out in the
Stadium, a dart arena with power-ups and its own race track, or play the house together: the toy soldiers, the
breakable props and the pickups are the same for everyone, and doors take the whole party along.

**In your hands:** a dart pistol, SMG and shotgun, a toy knife, a foam finger, grenades, a paper-plane glider and a
flashlight.

**Controls** (keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad; **H** in the game shows every key):
- WASD move, mouse look, Space jump (hold for higher), Left Shift run, C crouch
- E use, pick up, get in a car, shift at a core; F shift anywhere (it uses the charge; cores refill it)
- Left click fire or use; right click aim; R reload; G throw a grenade
- 1 to 4 the inventory slots; X holster, or put down what you hold; Z drop the weapon
- Left Ctrl dodge roll; Q block; V first person
- Online: Tab shows the scores. Anywhere in the game: F12 saves a bug note with a screenshot. Esc pauses.

**Made for** GitHub Game Off 2026 (theme: Shift) in Unity 6.

## Made with AI (be exact: this is what itch's question is about)
- **Code:** written with Claude Code (Anthropic), an AI coding tool he directed, playtested and reviewed.
- **On-screen text:** most menus, hints, prompts and labels were written by the same tool ✍ (name any you wrote or
  rewrote yourself).
- **Made by script:** a few small pieces were made by scripts that tool wrote, not by an image, sound or 3D
  generator: the shift orb, the portal ring and the nitro star models, a spark particle texture, and the arms-carry
  pose ✍ (drop any you replaced).
- **Made by AI through a script:** the title menu's 3D slabs (D-0196): made by ChatGPT writing Blender Python, to a
  layout he directed, then regrouped into pages and given their frames by a script Claude wrote (D-0197). No AI
  image or texture is in them.
- **AI-assisted:** the pixel title on the menu (D-0189). Its letter design comes from a mock-up Claude drew by
  script; he drew it into Aseprite from that ✍ (say how the pink and purple colours and the light sweep were made:
  by hand, by a script, or with another AI tool).
  On the menu each letter is now a block shape made from that sheet by a script Claude wrote (one box a pixel), and
  its four other colour sets are the same sheet with every hue turned by that script (D-0200): none of it is painted.
- **Made by AI (stand-ins):** the Flat Zone's two rifts (D-0207): the pixel rift was drawn by a script Claude wrote, in
  the title's colours; the 3D rift was modelled by a script Claude wrote in Blender, with his warp drawing inside. ✍ He
  plans his own versions: update this line when they replace them.
- **Not AI:** the graffiti title drawing (on the end card) and the portal and orb drawings are his. Every other model, character, animation and
  sound comes from the licensed packs in the credits.
